StrangeCraft Digital is an independent CGI studio specializing in film, television, commercials, and video game content. We bring extensive expertise across both pre-production and post-production — from consulting, design, and concept development to filming and the creation of high-quality animations.

Based in Chisinau, Moldova, our international team unites talented artists from Italy, Croatia, the UK, Romania, and Moldova. In an increasingly AI-driven world, we stand out by adding a distinctly human touch — blending technology with artistry to create work that feels alive.
